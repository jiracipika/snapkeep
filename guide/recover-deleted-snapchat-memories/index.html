Can You Recover Deleted Snapchat Memories?

Last updated October 2026 · Free · Honest answer inside

You deleted a Memory — or a whole batch — and now you want it back. Here's the honest answer up front: Snapchat has no trash can for Memories. A deleted Memory is removed from Snapchat's servers, and no app, service, or support ticket can bring it back. But "usually gone" isn't "always gone" — depending on where the copy you lost actually was, one of the recovery paths below may still work. Start with the one that matches your situation.

Path 1 — The Memory is still in Snapchat, but gone from your camera roll

This one always works. If the snap still appears in Memories (swipe up from the camera), you haven't lost it — it was only ever saved inside Snapchat, never to your phone. Open the snap, tap the export/save button, and choose Save to Camera Roll (or Export Snap). Do this for anything important — or skip the finger-ache: export your whole Memories archive in one request and convert it into dated gallery files.

Path 2 — You deleted the files from your phone's gallery

Photos and videos saved to your camera roll are ordinary files, and your phone's own deleted-photos folder is the best recovery chance you'll ever get:

  • iPhone: Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted — files stay recoverable for about 30 days.
  • Android / Google Photos: Photos app → Library → Trash — roughly 60 days before automatic purge.
  • Cloud backups: check iCloud Photos or Google Photos' archive and trash on the web too — the copies there are independent of your phone.

Path 3 — You downloaded a data export, then deleted the files

If you requested a My Data export from Snapchat at any point before the loss, that ZIP still contains the media from when it was made — check your downloads folder, external drives, and email for the Snapchat download link. The archive is awkward to read (machine-named files, missing dates, split videos); here's a map of what's inside and how to turn it back into watchable, dated photos and videos.

Path 4 — The Memory was deleted inside Snapchat

This is the hard one. Deleting a Memory in the app deletes it from Snapchat's servers immediately — there is no 30-day grace, no hidden folder, and Snapchat support cannot restore it. If the snap was also saved to your camera roll or included in a past data export, Paths 1–3 are your routes. If it lived only in Memories and you deleted it there, it's gone. Anyone who tells you otherwise — see the scam warning below — is selling hope.

Scam alert. Apps and "recovery services" that promise to restore deleted Snapchat Memories — especially ones asking for your Snapchat username and password — are scams or phishing. Snapchat's own help docs confirm deleted Memories are unrecoverable. Never hand your Snapchat credentials to a third-party "recovery" site.

Path 5 — The account itself was deleted

If a Snapchat account was deleted and the 30-day deactivation window has passed, everything inside it — including Memories — is permanently gone from Snapchat's side. The only surviving copy is one you made earlier: a data export on a drive, converted files in a gallery, a cloud backup. If you're currently inside that 30-day window, just log back in — the account and its Memories are restored. And if you're planning deletion, back up your Memories first — that order matters.

Make sure you never need this page

Every permanently-lost-Memory story ends the same way: there was exactly one copy and it lived in one place. Two cheap habits fix that permanently:

  • Export your archive now, not after something goes wrong. One request at accounts.snapchat.com, one download, one drop into Snapkeep — and your Memories are ordinary, dated files you can back up like any other photos.
  • Keep two copies in two places — phone or computer plus a cloud drive. That's the whole strategy.

Questions people ask

How do I recover deleted Snapchat Memories without a computer?

Check the phone's Recently Deleted album (iOS, ~30 days) or Google Photos trash (Android, ~60 days) first — that's where camera-roll copies survive. Memories deleted inside Snapchat are not recoverable from the phone or anywhere else.

Can Snapchat support restore deleted Memories?

No. Snapchat's support docs are explicit that deleted Memories cannot be recovered, and support cannot bypass that. Any service claiming otherwise is a scam.

Are deleted Snapchat Memories stored anywhere for 30 days?

That rumor gets repeated a lot, but no — Snapchat does not hold a hidden 30-day copy of deleted Memories. The only grace period Snapchat has is the 30-day account-deactivation window, which restores a whole account if you log back in, not individual deleted snaps.

Can police or forensic tools recover deleted Snapchat Memories?

Snapchat retains some data for legal-response purposes, and law enforcement can request what exists through official channels — that's not a process individuals can use. For a deleted personal photo you regret losing, assume it's gone and start the backup habit today.
